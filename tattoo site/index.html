Preservation Protocol

TATTOO AFTERCARE GUIDE

Flawless long-term healing requires disciplined care during the initial 14 days following your session.

01

Initial Wrap (24 hrs)

Keep the dermal second-skin film applied for 24 hours. Avoid picking or removing unless excessive fluid pooling occurs.

02

Gentle Cleansing

Wash with lukewarm water and fragrance-free antibacterial soap. Pat dry with clean paper towels—never rub or scratch.

03

Hydrate Lightly

Apply a thin layer of our provided AURA Balm 2-3 times daily. Excess ointment will clog pores and compromise ink retention.

04

Protect & Maintain

Avoid soaking in baths/pools, direct sunlight, and intense gym sessions for 14 days. Always apply SPF 50+ once fully healed.
